A CV and portfolio open doors.

## CV sections

- Summary;
- Skills (honest);
- Projects (with links);
- Experience;
- Education.

## Portfolio

GitHub with projects: description, screenshots, README.

## What matters

Real code, tests, docs, deployment.

## Lesson summary

Show working projects; keep CV honest and focused.

