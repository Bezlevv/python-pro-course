The community speeds up learning.

## Where

- Local meetups;
- PyCon and regional conferences;
- Online forums and chats;
- Open-source projects.

## How to start

- Answer questions;
- Report issues;
- Improve documentation;
- Small PRs.

## Lesson summary

Participate by helping and sharing.
